How to cancel

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, so canceling happens in your iPhone's settings, not in Poise.

  1. Open Settings and tap your name at the top.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Poise, then Cancel Subscription.

A canceled subscription keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for. During a free trial, cancel at least a day before it ends and nothing is charged. Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Common questions

Where do my messages live?

On your phone. Poise has no accounts.

What does Refine do with my message?

Refine runs only when you tap it and needs a connection. The message and your direction go to an outside partner to help find the right words. The final words are yours.

Where are my drafts?

In Drafts, under Sleeping on it or Still writing. An unfinished message stays put if your phone locks or restarts.

Is Poise therapy?

No. Poise is a place to write and take a moment. It isn't therapy, counseling, or medical advice.
