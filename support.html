How to cancel

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, so canceling happens in your iPhone's settings, not in Poise.

  1. Open Settings and tap your name at the top.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Poise, then Cancel Subscription.

A canceled subscription keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for. During a free trial, cancel at least a day before it ends and nothing is charged. Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A few answers

Where do my messages live? On your phone. Poise has no accounts.

What does Refine do with my message? Refine runs only when you tap it and needs a connection. It sends the message and your direction out, brings back one suggestion, and that's the end of it. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Where are my drafts? In Drafts, under Sleeping on it or Still writing. An unfinished message stays put if your phone locks or restarts.

Is Poise therapy? No. Poise is a place to write and take a moment. It isn't therapy, counseling, or medical advice.
