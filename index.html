How it works

Write

A quiet, private place for the messages that matter. No typing dots on the other end.

Refine

Know what you mean but not how to say it? Refine helps you find the right words, in your voice, and tells you what changed and why.

Reflect

Tap Reflect and the message is out of your hands for a moment, as if you'd sent it. Read it the way the other person will. Then send it, or go back and change it.

Inside iMessage

Use Poise without leaving the conversation. Not ready to reply yet? Send a pause, a one-line note that says you're taking a moment.

To find it, tap the plus beside the message field, then Poise.

The basics

Is Poise a messaging app?

It's the step before sending. You write in Poise, take your moment, then send straight from Poise through whichever app you already use. Your conversations stay where they are.

Is what I write private?

Yes. There are no accounts, and what you write stays on your phone. The one exception is Refine: when you tap it, Poise shares it with an outside partner to help find the right words.

What does it cost?

The first 14 days are free. After that, Poise is $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year, billed by Apple. Cancel in your iPhone's Settings anytime.

What do I need?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later. That's Apple's 2023 software, so any iPhone from 2018 on is fine.
