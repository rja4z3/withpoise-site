A moment before every message.

There's time to say it well. Poise holds a message for a breath before it goes, in the app or right inside iMessage.

Write

Write on a plain canvas. Tag who it's for and set an intention.

Reflect

Reflect feels like sending, before you send. Poise holds the message for 10, 20, or 30 seconds. Then you choose: Send, Edit message, Save as draft, or Let go.

Send

Send opens the share sheet. Inside iMessage, Poise sits under any conversation, so you can draft, refine, or send a pause without leaving the thread.

Refine

Refine is help finding the words. Choose Softer, Shorter, or Firmer, and it brings back one suggestion, with a line on why. Your meaning, your voice. Then Use this or Keep mine.
