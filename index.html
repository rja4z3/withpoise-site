How it works

Write

Write on a plain canvas. Tag who it's for and set an intention.

Refine

Know what you mean, but not how to say it? Ask for shorter, softer, or firmer, and Refine brings back one suggestion with a line on why. Your meaning, your voice. Use it or keep yours.

Reflect

Reflect feels like sending, before you send. Poise holds your message for 10, 20, or 30 seconds. Then it's your call: send it, edit it, save it for later, or let it go.

Inside iMessage

Poise sits under any conversation, so you can draft, refine, or send a pause without leaving the thread. Everywhere else, Send passes your message to the app you're using.

A few answers

Does Poise see my messages?

No. Poise has no accounts, and what you write stays on your phone.

What about Refine?

Refine runs only when you tap it and needs a connection. It sends the message and your direction out, brings back one suggestion, and that's the end of it. The Privacy Policy has the details.

What happens after the trial?

The first 14 days are free. After that, Poise is $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year, billed by Apple. Cancel in your iPhone's Settings anytime. Cancel at least a day before the trial ends and nothing is charged.
