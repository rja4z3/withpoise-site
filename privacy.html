Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Overview

This policy describes what information Poise handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. Poise is made by With Poise LLC.

Information Poise Handles

  • What you write, including messages, drafts, moments, intentions, notes, and tags, is stored on your device. Poise has no accounts.
  • When you use Refine, the message and your direction go through Poise's server to Anthropic, the outside partner that returns the suggestion. Refine requests carry an anonymous app identifier used to prevent abuse and enforce fair-use limits. They never carry your name, contacts, or message history.
  • Subscriptions are managed through an outside provider using an anonymous identifier, with payment handled by Apple.
  • To improve Poise, we collect usage analytics through an outside provider: which screens are visited and which features are used, along with basic device details (model, iOS version, app version) and approximate location estimated from your internet address, under a random identifier. What you write is never part of analytics or crash reports.
  • If Poise crashes, a crash report goes to the same analytics provider the next time the app opens: what failed inside the app, plus the same device details under the same random identifier.
  • The withpoise.app website counts visits through an outside provider, without cookies and without identifying you.
  • App preferences and permissions you grant (such as notifications) are stored on your device.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide Poise's features: rewriting a message when you tap Refine, managing your subscription, and delivering reminders you schedule. It's also used to understand how and where Poise is used, so we can improve it and decide where to focus.

Service Providers

Poise uses outside providers to process Refine requests (Anthropic), manage subscriptions, collect usage analytics and crash reports, and handle payments and app distribution. Each processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Retention and Deletion

Content you create stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app (subject to any device backups you have enabled). Refine requests are processed to return a result. Service providers retain records per their own policies.

Your Choices

  • Refine runs only when you tap it
  • Notifications can be turned off in Settings
  • Settings → Delete all data removes everything you've written from this device
  • Deleting the app removes the content stored on your device

Children

Poise is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Poise evolves. The date above reflects the latest revision, and material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
